# Alex Hormozi Offers Framework ($100M Offers & Scale Advisory)

This rulebook trains the **Script Writer** and **Content Planner** on how to structure the Sena Academy enrollment offer to maximize value and minimize friction.

---

## 1. The Value Equation

To make the Sena Academy offer irresistible, every content piece must maximize the numerator and minimize the denominator of the Value Equation:

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood of Achievement}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$$

### A. Dream Outcome (Maximize)
- Clearly define *what* the student gets. Avoid dry descriptions like "Learn JavaScript".
- Focus on outcomes: *Building your own SaaS product*, *landing a remote tech job*, *freelancing for global clients*, or *graduating with a portfolio of live websites*.

### B. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Maximize)
- Address the fear that "this won't work for me."
- Highlight:
  - Guided debugging: Students never get stuck alone.
  - Cohort structure: Peer motivation.
  - Active mentors: Immediate feedback on assignments.

### C. Time Delay (Minimize to Zero)
- Showcase how fast a student gets a win.
- Highlight the "Day 1 Win": *Writing your first HTML lines and launching a live webpage within 20 minutes.*
- Explain that they build real projects immediately, rather than reading theories for weeks.

### D. Effort & Sacrifice (Minimize to Zero)
- Detail how we remove friction.
- Highlight:
  - No complex environment setup: They code directly in the browser or cloud environments on day one.
  - Byte-sized lessons: Fits into busy exam schedules.
  - Mentors holding them accountable so they don't have to self-teach in a vacuum.

---

## 2. Offer Stacking & Bonuses

When scriptwriting calls-to-action or structuring landing page copy, present the program as an **Offer Stack**:

1.  **Core Curriculum**: Full-Stack Web Development Course (Structure, coding, deployment).
2.  **Bonus 1 (Community)**: Private WhatsApp/Discord Cohort Group (Peer support and live feedback).
3.  **Bonus 2 (Mentorship)**: Live weekly office hours and Q&A sessions with senior devs.
4.  **Bonus 3 (Templates & Boilerplates)**: Pre-built templates so they can launch projects faster.
5.  **Bonus 4 (Portfolio Builder)**: Assistance in hosting and styling their personal site.

---

## 3. Scriptwriting Application Rules

- **The Contrast Rule**: Never just state the price. Stack the total value (e.g. *"$1,500 of mentorship, curriculum, and community"*), then show the actual cost.
- **Urgency & Scarcity**: Emphasize that cohort sizes are strictly capped to ensure high mentor-to-student ratios.
- **Risk Reversal**: Frame the enrollment with zero risk (e.g. waitlist access, or money-back guarantees).
