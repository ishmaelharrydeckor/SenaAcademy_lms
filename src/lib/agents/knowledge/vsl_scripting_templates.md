# VSL Scripting Templates & Copywriting Rules (Hormozi & Sugarman)

This rulebook trains the **Script Writer** on the exact structural pacing and writing flow for short-form videos and Video Sales Letters (VSLs).

---

## 1. The Slippery Slide (Joseph Sugarman)

The sole purpose of the first sentence is to get you to read the second sentence. Every paragraph must flow seamlessly into the next, making it impossible for the viewer to stop reading or watching.

### A. Curiosity Hooks & Transitions
Use "seeds of curiosity" at the end of sections to keep interest high:
- *"But there was a catch..."*
- *"And that's when everything changed."*
- *"Here is the part nobody tells you..."*

### B. Plain Language
Avoid complex academic terminology or marketing buzzwords. Write exactly how people speak. Use active verbs and concrete nouns.

---

## 2. The $100M VSL Script Framework (Alex Hormozi)

Every short-form video script must be broken into two columns: **Visual (what the viewer sees)** and **Audio (what the speaker says)**. The sequence follows this progression:

### Step 1: The Attention Hook (0 - 3 seconds)
- Interrupt their scroll by challenging a common belief or showing a painful situation.
- *Visual*: High energy, showing the terminal, dashboard, or code problem immediately.

### Step 2: Identify the Enemy / Agitate (3 - 10 seconds)
- Define the core problem (e.g. self-doubt, expensive programs, getting stuck on syntax).
- *Audio*: *"You buy a 40-hour course, get stuck on line 12 because of a syntax error, and nobody is there to help you. So you quit."*

### Step 3: Introduce the Guide & Solution (10 - 20 seconds)
- Present the solution as the only logical way out.
- *Audio*: *"That's why at Sena Academy, we don't do pre-recorded lectures. You build projects live, and a senior mentor debugs your code in real-time."*

### Step 4: Value Stack & Risk Reversal (20 - 45 seconds)
- List the bonuses (Curriculum + Community + Live Q&As + Portfolio).
- Address the risk: *"You only pay when you are ready, or join the waitlist completely free."*

### Step 5: Direct Call to Action (45 - 60 seconds)
- Tell them exactly what to do. Use plain verbs: *Join*, *Click*, *Enroll*.
- *Audio*: *"Go to senaacademy.org and join the August cohort waitlist now."*

---

## 3. Visual & Audio Column Formatting Rule
The output script must format as a structured markdown table:

| Timeline | Visual Direction (Camera action, text overlays, sound effects) | Audio Script (Voiceover, spoken words) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:03** | Zoomed in on a laptop screen showing red terminal errors. Text overlay: "Why you quit coding." | *"This is the exact reason most people quit learning how to code."* |
| **0:03 - 0:10** | Camera pans to speaker nodding. Zoom cut. | *"It's not because you're bad at math. It's because you got stuck on a trailing comma..."* |
